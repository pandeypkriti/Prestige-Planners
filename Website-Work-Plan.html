4The conversion flow chart · client-facing deliverable

The team's biggest ask: a flow chart / board that walks Benafsha through how a cold visitor becomes a booked lead, and what each section and button is doing for her conversions. Presented side-by-side with her old site, pitch-deck style. Draft of the journey it will show:

COLD LEAD · doesn't know her yet

Arrives from a Meta ad, Google search ("wedding planner Dubai"), or Instagram · most likely on a phone

Five Stages of Awareness: they have a problem (an event to plan), zero trust in her yet. The site's job is to warm them up step by step.

HERO · first impression

Cinematic luxury opening + headline that answers what / niche / where in one line + a visible "Check availability" CTA

"If they can make a stunning hero, they must know design." In a creative industry the website IS the proof of taste. Impatient visitors can convert immediately.

FOUNDER REVEAL · the trust moment

Benafsha's photo rises and expands into her story

People don't trust companies, they trust people. One-to-one contact with the person in charge = accountability = trust built early.

STATS DECK · proof at a glance

Numbers cycle automatically, each pulling up a real event record card

Track record without reading a word. Detail callouts show her attention to detail: the thing clients actually buy.

PORTFOLIO STACK · show, don't tell

Full-bleed real event photos, each with its own "Enquire about this event" button

A bride looking for a planner wants to SEE weddings they've done. Every card is also an exit ramp straight to WhatsApp.

SERVICES · "they do what I need"

Auto-cycling accordion: Planning / Styling / Coordination

Answers "can they handle MY kind of event?" without a menu dive.

SHOWCASE FILM · the immersion beat

Luxury footage scales to fullscreen mid-scroll, autoplaying

Motion sells atmosphere in a way photos can't. Future home of her own filmed content.

TESTIMONIALS + FAQ · objections handled

Auto-rotating client stories, then swipeable answers to the questions every host asks

By now they trust her taste and record; this clears the final doubts (budget, timing, scope) before the ask.

ONE CLEAR ACTION · the invitation

Single metallic-gold WhatsApp CTA + pulsating WhatsApp float that never left the corner

Zero hunting for contact. WhatsApp-first because that's how Dubai actually books. The visitor was hand-held from stranger → enquiry.

AFTER SIGNATURE · the upsell pathway

Buttons route to GHL/Calendly booking → synced calendar slot → automatic email + SMS reminders → post-meeting follow-ups → lead-nurture funnel (CRM)

The website becomes the gateway to a full sales system. This is the "beyond the website" vision we sell on the call.

5Pitch structure and talking points

The four broad categories (Kynan's structure · don't script, just know these)

  • 1 · What we did · visual uplift, real portfolio, founder forward, mobile-first
  • 2 · The purpose of the website · convert cold ad traffic into WhatsApp enquiries
  • 3 · What each section does · walk the flow chart
  • 4 · How it helps HER · more booked leads, less friction, a system not a brochure
The money line: "You made six figures last year from 15–20 booked leads, all word of mouth, none from online. Imagine doubling that. To run ads you need somewhere for the ads to send people: a website built to convert. This is that website."
  • Five Stages of Awareness framing (Mulla): the site walks a stranger from "I have an event" to "I trust this person with it."
  • Design = credibility: in event planning, a creative industry, a stunning site is itself proof of capability.
  • Mobile stat: majority of her traffic will arrive by phone; we designed phone-first.
  • Old vs new CTAs: her old site made interested visitors hunt for contact; now every portfolio card, the nav, the float and the finale all route to WhatsApp.
  • Beyond the website: GHL calendar, auto reminders, nurture funnel · paint the full pathway, sell the vision of a sales system.
  • Positioning note: she said "I thought we were leaving the website for a while and focusing on IG." Frame this as exactly that: no pressure, "we just wanted to show you what's possible" · the IG content Mulla is filming plugs INTO this site later.

7Call prep rules · Kynan's coaching

  • Conversation, not script. Slides/board carry minimal text; the substance comes from you talking. "No one wants to listen to a script, they want a conversation."
  • Know the four categories, let the details come naturally · you built it, you know the product best.
  • Never defend. If she criticises: "Our rationale was X, but I totally see your point · you know your business best, happy to adjust."
  • Drop the fear. If this one doesn't land, more clients are lined up; it still goes in the portfolio either way ("international client, Dubai" is already a portfolio win).
  • Walk her through the WHY, not just the pretty. "You can make the sexiest website in the world · if it doesn't convert, it means nothing."
  • Plant the idea: she doesn't yet know she needs it. The flow chart is the planting tool.
