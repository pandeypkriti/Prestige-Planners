Prestige Planners · team briefing

Kriti's run sheet for the team call · project status, findings, and the road ahead

00The 30-second opener

"Prestige Planners is our first paying client: a solo luxury event planner in Dubai, Benafsha. Her events are genuinely beautiful, but her online presence doesn't sell them. We audited everything, extracted what was valuable, and built her a luxury landing page designed to convert Meta-ads traffic into WhatsApp enquiries. Today I'll demo it, show you what's left, and where we take her next."

01The client, in one minute

  • Who: Prestige Planners · luxury wedding & event planning, Dubai (Meydan Grandstand). Solo founder: Benafsha Engineer-Mulla (ex-aviation + hospitality). New to tech and marketing.
  • The deal: Meta-ads-optimised landing page → Calendly/WhatsApp booking → CRM + lead automations. Strategy: ship a showcase landing page first to lock in trust, then upsell the full build.
  • Where she is now: basic website, new Instagram (~267 followers), no ads running, strong Google rating that nobody sees.

02Her weak points · what the audit found

GapWhy it costs her sales

03What we extracted from her

  • Brand kit from her guidelines PDF: navy #002F5F · gold #C4A24C · cream #F3ECDC, translated her paid fonts into web-safe Fraunces + Poppins.
  • Real event photography: pulled, upscaled, and art-directed her strongest assets (champagne tower wall, floral walkway, black & gold glamour, garden shower, banquets).
  • Motion from stills: turned her static photos into cinemagraph-style ambient video so the page feels alive without fake stock footage.
  • Her proof: Google rating + review language, event types, guest counts · turned into the stats and story cards on the page.
  • A design language: researched real luxury-editorial references and locked a reusable system (gilded navy buttons, italic serif labels, glass chips, grain, gold hairlines). This is now OUR house style for luxury clients, not just hers.

04What I built · demo walkthrough

Share the screen, open Prestige-Planners-Preview.html, and scroll top to bottom. Signature moments to call out:

Demo pointers (in scroll order)

  • Hero: layered cinematic scroll · the arch rises through a growing gold glow while the title sinks behind it, over a blurred ambient video. Note the drawing hairlines + cascading dateline.
  • Stats spotlight: rolling odometer numbers; the three stats take turns in focus while the rest blur, and the Polaroid deck pulls each stat's story card forward in sync. Hover a number: it grabs its card.
  • Blur-to-focus manifesto: words sharpen as you scroll, golden glimmers drift up, ambient video breathes underneath.
  • Portfolio stack: full-bleed event cards glide up and stack, each with its own WhatsApp enquiry CTA.
  • Services accordion: auto-cycles through Planning / Styling / Coordination every 10s, click to expand.
  • Dark champagne panel: shining outline words sway left/right behind the video, which scales to fullscreen as you scroll. Slim Instagram follow link rides the bottom.
  • Testimonials: auto-rotating spotlight, one story at a time, word-by-word reveal with gold highlights.
  • FAQ coverflow: question boards angled like a photo gallery · arrows, dots, drag, or keyboard to flip through.
  • Final CTA: gold frame draws itself, lines mask in, magnetic metallic-gold WhatsApp button. One CTA, no distractions.

Craft layer to mention: Lenis-style smooth scroll grain on every section reduced-motion support mobile pass done floating WhatsApp button availability modal optimised hero video 8.8MB → 224KB single-file shareable build
