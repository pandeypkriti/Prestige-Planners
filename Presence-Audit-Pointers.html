Prestige Planners — Online Presence Audit & Pointers

Fresh audit, July 2026. Founder: Benafsha Engineer-Mulla (ex-aviation + hospitality, London→Dubai, mum of two).

Surfaces reviewed: website (Home, Services, Wedding/portfolio, About, Contact, Blog), Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Linktree, + Google/TikTok/Pinterest/YouTube search.

✅ WHAT SHE ALREADY HAS GOING (strengths to build on)

  1. A real, good-looking luxury website with strong photography — most new solo planners don't have this. H1: "We curate bespoke moments, crafting timeless memories."
  2. WhatsApp done right — prominent, consistent button on every page. The #1 CTA for the Dubai market.
  3. A named, human founder story — Benafsha (aviation + hospitality, London→Dubai, mum of two). A genuine luxury differentiator.
  4. Social proof already exists — 7 five-star testimonials on the homepage.
  5. A substantial portfolio — ~30+ event images (Décor / F&B / Entertainment).
  6. Consistent luxury brand voice across website, Linktree, LinkedIn.
  7. Full contact infrastructure — working form, email, address, hours, Linktree hub.
  8. A blog framework with SEO-style category titles — the skeleton for content marketing.

⚠️ WHAT SHE NEEDS TO WORK ON (exists but weak/inconsistent)

  1. Fix template-artifact page titles (hurts luxury feel + SEO). Homepage reads generic "Event Agency"; Services is lowercase "services"; About reads "About Us — 1" (leftover slug). Rewrite to e.g. "Luxury Wedding & Event Planner in Dubai | Prestige Planners."
  2. The blog is stale — all 6 posts dated 20 June 2025, nothing since (~12 months). Either post ~1/month or hide the dates; a dormant blog signals an inactive business.
  3. Thin service copy — Services page is 10 icon tiles with near-placeholder text + generic FAQ. Add 2–3 real sentences per service + a real FAQ (lead times, budgets, travel, what's included).
  4. Portfolio is a photo dump, not proof — convert the best 3–4 into mini case studies (event type, guest count, venue, challenge, client quote).
  5. LinkedIn dormant — 6 followers, one post ~10 months old. Good "about" copy, no activity. Post 1–2x/month for corporate leads.
  6. Facebook link likely a personal profile, not a business Page (profile.php?id= pattern). A personal profile can't run ads or collect reviews — verify & migrate to a Page.
  7. Testimonials not attributed or spread out — only on homepage. Add names/event types (with permission); surface 1–2 on Services + Wedding pages.
  8. Fix the 404 orphan page — /about-event-management- is indexed by Google but dead. Redirect it so search traffic isn't leaking.
  9. Linktree incomplete — add a direct email button and (once created) TikTok/Pinterest.

🔧 WHAT SHE NEEDS IMPLEMENTED (missing entirely — add these)

  1. Claim/optimise the Google Business Profile — highest ROI. "Wedding planner near me" + Maps drive real Dubai enquiries. Complete category, hours, WhatsApp, 15–20 photos, review-request link. (Rating/review count unconfirmed — verify in Google Maps.)
  2. Pinterest business account — brides plan on Pinterest for months. Boards: Real Weddings, Tablescapes, Desert/Yacht Parties, Corporate Galas, pinned to the site. Currently absent.
  3. TikTok account — the #1 discovery engine for Dubai events now. Setups, reveals, BTS. Currently absent.
  4. Persistent "Enquire / Book a Consultation" button in the nav on every page (not just the floating WhatsApp).
  5. A dedicated filterable Portfolio page in the main nav (Weddings / Corporate / Showers / Yacht & Desert).
  6. A founder photo + 30-sec intro video on About — she has the story but no face to it. Big trust boost for a solo planner.
  7. Meta descriptions on every page (none detected) so Google snippets are controlled.
  8. Instagram Story Highlights (confirm with her): Real Weddings, Corporate, Showers & Parties, Client Love, Enquire.
  9. A packages / pricing signal — even "Packages from…" or a downloadable planning checklist to set expectations + capture emails. Currently zero pricing anywhere.
  10. Analytics — confirm Google Analytics / Meta Pixel are installed to measure conversions.

  • Facebook likes, recency, Page-vs-profile — FB login wall.
