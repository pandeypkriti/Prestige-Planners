⭐ v1 LANDING PAGE — the exact feature list to build now

One page, top to bottom. This is what I'll build:

  1. Sticky header — logo + a single gold "Enquire on WhatsApp" button
  2. Hero — full-bleed image, luxury headline, one subline, one WhatsApp CTA
  3. Trust bar — ★★★★★ · number of events · a venue/press mention
  4. Featured Events — 3 mini case-study cards (photo + type + guests + venue + quote)
  5. Services strip — Weddings · Showers · Corporate · Yacht & Desert (one line each)
  6. Founder Spotlight — Benafsha's photo + short story (the trust-closer)
  7. Testimonial carousel — named quotes with event types
  8. "How We Work" — 3 steps (Enquire → Consult → We handle everything)
  9. Closing CTA band — "Let's plan something extraordinary" + big WhatsApp button
  10. Footer — logo, WhatsApp, email, Instagram, discreet copyright
  11. Always-on: floating mobile WhatsApp bubble · pre-filled message · fade-on-scroll animation · flawless mobile · correct page title + favicon

Everything here is buildable with placeholder-but-tasteful imagery today, then swapped for her real photos.

The pitch power of this

When you show her the page, each section is a silent answer to a problem she just felt:

  • She felt "hard to book" → she sees the sticky WhatsApp button everywhere.
  • She felt "my gallery looks like stock" → she sees real captioned case studies.
  • She felt "people can't see me" → she sees her own face and story done beautifully.
  • She felt "it looks a bit template" → she sees something that looks like a high-end agency made it.

You're not describing features — you're resolving tensions you created 30 seconds earlier. That's what closes it.

The ⚠️/🔧 items that DON'T fit on one page (Pinterest, TikTok, Google Profile, full portfolio, blog, packages) are exactly your website upsell — the roadmap you present after she says yes to the page.
